FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreigners rent and drive in Rwanda?
Yes! You can drive with a valid driving licence from your home country or an International Driving Permit. Rwanda drives on the right-hand side. If you prefer not to drive, add one of our professional drivers for $20/day.
How do I pay if I don't have Mobile Money?
Foreign visitors can send the deposit to our MoMo number (+250 783 930 289) using remittance apps like WorldRemit, Remitly, Sendwave or TapTap Send — or simply reserve for free on WhatsApp and pay by MoMo or cash when we hand over the car in Kigali.
Is a deposit required?
A 30% deposit via MTN Mobile Money confirms your booking and locks the car for your dates. The balance is payable at handover. Cancellations 48+ hours before pickup get a full deposit refund.
Do you deliver to Kigali International Airport?
Yes — airport delivery and collection are completely free, at any hour. Send us your flight number and we'll track your arrival and be waiting with the car, even if the flight is delayed.
Which car is best for gorilla trekking / national parks?
For Volcanoes National Park and Akagera we recommend a 4x4: the Land Cruiser Prado TX (best all-rounder), Land Cruiser V8 (maximum comfort) or the Safari Jeep (open-roof game drives). The roads to park headquarters can be rough, so ground clearance matters.
Is insurance included?
Yes, all our vehicles carry comprehensive insurance. We'll explain the simple terms (and the small excess) clearly on WhatsApp before you commit — no fine-print surprises.
Can I take the car outside Rwanda?
Cross-border trips to Uganda or Tanzania are possible on request with prior arrangement (COMESA insurance extension required). Message us on WhatsApp with your route and we'll sort the paperwork.
